ITEM 1. BUSINESS

Overview

Apex Technologies Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal
computers, tablets, wearables, accessories, and related services. The Company
sells its products worldwide through its retail stores, online stores, and direct
sales force, as well as through third-party cellular network carriers, wholesalers,
retailers, and resellers. The Company's fiscal year ends on the last Saturday of
September.

Products and Services

The Company manages its business primarily on a geographic basis. The reportable
segments of the Company consist of the Americas, Europe, Greater China, Japan, and
Rest of Asia Pacific. A significant portion of the Company's revenue and earnings is
denominated in foreign currencies, which subjects the Company to foreign exchange risk.

Smartphones (Apex Phone)
Apex Phone is the Company's line of smartphones based on the Company's iOS operating
system. As of the end of fiscal 2023, the Apex Phone product line accounted for the
largest share of the Company's net sales.

Personal Computers (Apex Desktop and Laptop)
The Company's Mac line of personal computers includes laptops and desktops based on the
Company's macOS operating system.

Tablets (Apex Pad)
Apex Pad is the Company's line of tablets based on iOS. It is available in multiple
sizes and configurations.

Wearables, Home, and Accessories
This segment includes Apex Watch, Apex AirPods, Apex TV, and a variety of accessories
including cases, cables, and adapters.

Services
The Company's services offerings include digital content stores and streaming services,
licensing, and other services. Services revenue includes revenue from the Company's App
Store, Apple Music-equivalent subscription streaming, iCloud-equivalent cloud storage,
advertising services, and payment services.

Research and Development

The Company believes that focused investments in research and development are critical
to its future growth and competitive position in the marketplace and are directly
related to timely development of new and enhanced products and services. The Company
continues to develop new technologies to enhance existing products and to expand the
range of its product offerings through research and development, licensing of
intellectual property, and acquisition of third-party businesses and technology. The
Company's research and development expenditure was $29.9 billion in fiscal 2023,
compared to $26.3 billion in fiscal 2022 and $21.9 billion in fiscal 2021. The
Company employs a world-class team of engineers and scientists across hardware,
software, silicon design, machine learning, and services.

The Company's R&D strategy centers on vertical integration: designing its own silicon
chips (the Apex Silicon series), tightly coupled operating systems, and services. This
approach gives the Company greater control over performance, power efficiency, and
security relative to competitors who rely on third-party chip vendors. Investments in
fiscal 2023 were particularly concentrated in augmented reality hardware, next-
generation silicon architecture, and machine learning inference capabilities for on-
device processing.

Competition

The markets for the Company's products and services are highly competitive and subject
to rapid technological change and evolving industry standards. The Company competes
with companies that have significant resources, broad product lines, technical expertise,
and established customer relationships. The Company believes that its differentiation
through tightly integrated hardware and software, a strong ecosystem of third-party
applications, brand loyalty, and a growing services revenue base provides a competitive
advantage. However, the Company faces intense competition on price, especially in
emerging markets where lower-cost Android-based devices are popular.

Capital Return Program

The Company has an active program to return value to its shareholders through dividends
and share repurchases. During fiscal 2023, the Company repurchased $77.6 billion of its
common stock and paid $15.0 billion in dividends. The Board of Directors has authorized
a share repurchase program of up to $90 billion, of which $84.4 billion remained
available as of September 30, 2023. The Company intends to increase dividends annually
subject to declaration by the Board of Directors. The Company uses free cash flow
generated from operations as the primary funding source for its capital return program
and does not intend to take on significant incremental debt to fund buybacks.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS
================================================================================

The following risk factors could materially adversely affect the Company's business,
financial condition, and results of operations.

Global Supply Chain Concentration and Disruption Risk

The Company relies significantly on single-source suppliers and contract manufacturers
concentrated primarily in Asia, particularly China, Taiwan, and Vietnam. A substantial
portion of the Company's products is assembled in China by Foxconn Technology Group and
Pegatron Corporation. Any disruption to the Company's supply chain resulting from natural
disasters, geopolitical events, labour actions, public health crises, or government
restrictions on trade could materially impair the Company's ability to produce and deliver
products to customers on time.

In fiscal 2023, the Company accelerated its supplier diversification strategy, adding
manufacturing capacity in India and Vietnam. However, the transition is multi-year and the
Company remains heavily dependent on its existing concentration of suppliers. Component
shortages — particularly in advanced semiconductors, display panels, and memory chips —
could force production cuts and result in lost revenue. The Company has invested in
long-term supply agreements and strategic inventory buffers to partially mitigate this
risk, but these measures cannot fully protect against large-scale disruptions.

Geopolitical and Regulatory Risk

The Company is subject to complex and evolving laws and regulations in the U.S. and
internationally, including export controls, trade sanctions, data privacy regulations,
anti-trust scrutiny, and app store regulations. In fiscal 2023, the Company faced
antitrust investigations by the European Commission regarding its App Store practices
and related third-party payment restrictions. The Digital Markets Act and equivalent
legislation in other jurisdictions may require the Company to permit alternative app
stores and payment processors, which could reduce the Company's Services revenue.
Separately, escalating geopolitical tensions between the United States and China
represent a risk to the Company's supply chain, retail operations, and market access
in Greater China, which contributed approximately 19% of the Company's net sales in
fiscal 2023.

Technology and Intellectual Property Risk

The Company's business depends substantially on the continuing development of new and
innovative hardware and software products. Failure to keep pace with technological
change, or to successfully integrate emerging technologies such as augmented reality
and next-generation wireless standards into compelling products, could cause the
Company to lose market share. The Company may also become subject to patent or other
intellectual property claims that could require the Company to pay substantial damages
or to alter its products.

Cybersecurity Risk

The Company's products and services process, store, and transmit large amounts of
personal and sensitive user data. A significant breach of the Company's systems or of
the systems of the Company's suppliers, partners, or third-party app developers could
expose the Company to regulatory penalties, reputational damage, and litigation. The
Company has invested substantially in security architecture and incident response
capabilities, but no system is immune to sophisticated attacks.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION
AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Overview

The following discussion should be read together with the consolidated financial
statements and accompanying notes. All references to "fiscal 2023," "fiscal 2022,"
and "fiscal 2021" mean the Company's fiscal years ended September 30, 2023,
September 24, 2022, and September 25, 2021, respectively.

Fiscal 2023 Highlights

Net sales were $383.3 billion for fiscal 2023, a decrease of 3% compared to net sales
of $394.3 billion for fiscal 2022. The decrease was primarily driven by lower net sales
in the Products segment, partially offset by higher net sales in Services. Net income
was $97.0 billion for fiscal 2023, compared to $99.8 billion for fiscal 2022.

Segment Performance

Americas segment net sales were $162.1 billion for fiscal 2023, compared to $169.7
billion for fiscal 2022, a decrease of 4%.

Europe segment net sales were $94.3 billion for fiscal 2023, compared to $95.1 billion
for fiscal 2022, a decrease of 1%.

Greater China segment net sales were $72.6 billion for fiscal 2023, compared to $74.2
billion for fiscal 2022, a decrease of 2%.

Japan segment net sales were $24.3 billion for fiscal 2023, compared to $25.5 billion
for fiscal 2022, a decrease of 5%.

Rest of Asia Pacific segment net sales were $29.6 billion for fiscal 2023, compared to
$29.4 billion for fiscal 2022, an increase of 1%.

Outlook

The Company does not provide specific forward-looking guidance on revenue or earnings
per share. However, the Company notes that it expects year-over-year revenue performance
in the December 2023 quarter to be similar to the September 2023 quarter growth rate.
The Company expects Services gross margins to remain above 70% for the foreseeable
future, driven by scale benefits and the high-margin nature of digital content licensing.
Foreign exchange headwinds are expected to continue to weigh on reported results given
the strength of the U.S. dollar.

ITEM 3. LEGAL PROCEEDINGS
================================================================================

Epic Games Litigation

In August 2020, Epic Games filed a lawsuit in the U.S. District Court for the Northern
District of California alleging that the Company's App Store policies violate federal
and state antitrust laws. The district court issued a ruling in September 2021 that
was partially favourable and partially unfavourable to both parties. Both parties
appealed. In fiscal 2023, the Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals largely affirmed the
district court ruling. The U.S. Supreme Court declined to hear the Company's appeal
on certain issues. The Company continues to believe its App Store policies are lawful
and is prepared to comply with applicable court orders.

European Commission Investigation

The European Commission opened a formal investigation into the Company's App Store
practices under the Digital Markets Act in March 2024. The Company is cooperating
fully with the investigation. A finding of non-compliance could result in substantial
fines and required changes to the Company's business model in the European Union.

Patent Litigation

The Company is involved in various patent disputes with competitors and non-practising
entities in multiple jurisdictions. The Company does not believe any currently pending
litigation will have a material adverse effect on its consolidated financial statements.

ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, AND GOVERNANCE
================================================================================

Environmental Commitments

The Company is committed to being carbon neutral across its entire value chain by 2030.
As of fiscal 2023, the Company's corporate operations have been carbon neutral since 2020.
The Company sources 100% of its electricity from renewable sources for its offices, data
centres, and retail stores globally. The Company is working with its manufacturing
suppliers to transition to clean energy and has committed to supplying clean energy to
over 300 suppliers through its Supplier Clean Energy Program. The Company has also
committed to eliminating plastics from packaging by 2025 and to using recycled and
responsibly sourced materials across all products.

Supply Chain Labour Standards

The Company maintains a Supplier Code of Conduct that sets standards for safe working
conditions, fair treatment of workers, environmental responsibility, and ethical business
practices. The Company conducts annual audits of its highest-risk suppliers and publishes
a Supplier Responsibility Progress Report annually.
